I am currently a forth-year Ph.D. candidate in the School of Computing at Clemson University, advised by [Prof. Long Cheng](https://people.computing.clemson.edu/~lcheng2/). I earned my M.S. in Information Science from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://www.sci.pitt.edu/) and my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from [Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://www.njupt.edu.cn/en/). Before joining Clemson University, I worked as a Data Engineer at Shein and Shopee for two years. My research interests lie in privacy and policy compliance across digital platforms, including IoT systems, mobile apps, and AI agent ecosystems, with an emphasis on privacy transparency, meaningful user control, and the alignment between system behavior and users' expectations.
